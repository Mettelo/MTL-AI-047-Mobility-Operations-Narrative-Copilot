## Delivery principle

The team creates **MTL-AI-047-<team-name>** under the Mettelo GitHub organisation and carries out all implementation, testing, evaluation, UAT, handover and final submission there.

The uploaded Bike Sharing source in the master is an approved reference input. Its presence does not mean data validation, KPI computation, model execution or acceptance has already been completed.

## Definition of complete

Completion requires executed evidence against D1–D12 and the acceptance criteria, deterministic KPI computation before AI generation, verified evidence-linked narratives, human review, UAT, independent rerun, technical handover and contribution evidence.

A dashboard, template narrative, screenshot or demo alone is not completion.
